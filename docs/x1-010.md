# Seta/Allumer X1-010

VGM 1.71 stores the X1-010 clock at header `0xD8`.  Register and memory writes
use command `0xC8` (`c8 aa aa vv`, big-endian 16-bit offset) and data block
`0x91` supplies the 1 MB sample ROM.

The chip has sixteen voices.  Each voice is either a 128-byte signed wavetable
voice with a 128-byte hardware volume envelope, or an 8-bit PCM voice reading
`start*0x1000 .. (0x100-end)*0x1000` from the ROM.

## Conversion

- Wavetable pitch uses the source 16-bit frequency register and Furnace's
  frequency-mode pitch table.
- The selected source waveform becomes a Furnace wavetable; repeated contents
  share one block.
- The selected envelope shape becomes a 128-entry, height-15 wavetable.  The
  source left/right nibbles are averaged because the module is mono.
- Envelope mode, shape and period become native X1-010 instrument macros.
- Held pitch slides whose register writes fit the row grid become `E8xx` /
  `E9xx` / `E5xx` pattern steps.  Only sub-row vibrato stays in the instrument
  pitch macro, so slides do not consume the 250-slot instrument budget or hit
  the 255-entry macro cap.
- PCM bodies are kept as exact source ranges and played through a 120-slot
  native sample map.  The source PCM frequency is emitted as `20xx`.
- PCM playback keys itself off at the ROM end address, matching VGMPlay.  A
  driver that writes key-on again after that point restarts the sample and
  becomes a new note, even when the status byte was never rewritten to 0.
- Source clocks of 16 MHz, 16.67 MHz and 14.32 MHz map to Furnace's standard
  X1-010 clock settings; other clocks use `customClock`.

## known limits

- X1-010 stereo output is collapsed to mono, so different envelope or PCM
  left/right nibbles become an average.
- A period-0 envelope follows live rewrites of its first byte as volume steps;
  other live envelope-shape rewrites use the note-start snapshot.
- Waveform RAM is snapshotted at note-on when a driver rewrites a waveform
  while that waveform is sounding.
- X1 pitch macros are kept linear rather than tail-loop searched, so driver
  slides do not become repeating LFO cycles.
- PCM playback is one-shot, matching the VGMPlay reference.
- PCM address ranges are quantized to the chip's 4 KB start/end registers.
- The module offers 1 MB of non-banked sample memory; banked logs that exceed it
  are reported and truncated.
- Command `0xC8` has no per-instance bit, so a second header-declared chip
  cannot receive separate writes.
